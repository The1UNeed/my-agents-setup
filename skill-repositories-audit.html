Cursor PStack and Matt Pocock's skills, evaluated against conversation history from the MacBook and the Mac Mini across Claude Code, Claudex, Codex, pi, grok build, opencode, and Cursor CLI, covering Fable 5, Opus 5, Sonnet 5, Opus 4.8, GPT‑5.6 Sol/Terra, GPT‑5.5, Kimi K3/K2.x, GLM‑5.2, and Grok 4.5/4.6.

Second pass, 20 August 2026. Supersedes the 19 August pass with a broader corpus: every harness on both machines, not only the Claude harness. No skills were installed and no configuration was changed.

Recommendation: selective hybrid, unchanged from the first pass, with a reordered shortlist. Matt Pocock's skills remain the more portable foundation; PStack holds the highest-leverage individual workflows. The broader corpus promotes arena (your cross-model design bake-off, productized), promotes automate-me (encode your design taste once instead of re-teaching every model), and upgrades git-guardrails from conditional to recommended given the full-YOLO configs and revert history found on the Mac Mini. Do not install either repository wholesale.

Strongest recurring signals

Four patterns dominate and are provable in every corpus:

PatternEvidence
Cross-model bake-offsIdentical briefs run through 4 to 6 models in parallel directories (OpenFlow‑Fable5 / ‑GPT5.5 / ‑Opus4.8 / ‑GLM; notics.top ×5 model dirs); "make 5 different but unique and minimalistic designs", then pick and expand a winner.
Adversarial multi-model reviewHand-written attestation prompts recur verbatim: exact-head PR comments from GPT‑5.6 Sol, Opus 5, and Kimi K3, each beginning PASS or FAIL and naming the exact SHA. Findings tagged by origin ("Fable-only:", "GPT-only:"); disagreements settled empirically by writing a test.
Delegate-implement, verify with proofCodex (GPT‑5.6 Sol) implements and verifies with exact commands and reporting contracts; Claude plans and reviews. The #1 frustration is unverified fixes: "It still occurred", "No it did nothing to the UI", a months-long half-screen iPadOS bug re-reported 5+ times.
Taste, not correctness, is the barThe most repeated correction across all harnesses is aesthetic: no card grids, no AI-cliché design, Apple HIG, animations originate at the point of touch, bilingual EN/zh‑CN mandatory, dictation stays verbatim. None of it is encoded anywhere, so every new model relearns it by being called ugly.

Use conditionally

CHERRY-PICK

PStack principles (5 of 21)

prove-it-works, fix-root-causes, laziness-protocol, guard-the-context-window, minimize-reader-load. Small, harness-neutral, aligned with your stated "concise, simple" preference. Skip the other 16 until a concrete need appears.

UPGRADED

git-guardrails-claude-code (Matt)

Raised from conditional this pass. Both machines run full YOLO (danger-full-access, approval_policy = "never", skipped permission prompts), secrets get pasted into prompts, and ~8 hard reverts were needed. A hook blocking destructive git before execution is cheap insurance where the orchestration actually happens.

GREENFIELD

architect (PStack) + codebase-design (Matt)

For OpenFlow/OpenLM-style projects where a big spec is pasted with "finish the whole project". Sketching types and module seams first reduces the mid-flight redesigns.

LARGE RUNS

figure-it-out (PStack)

For autonomous multi-part programs. Prefer it over Matt's wayfinder/to-tickets/triage, which assume an issue tracker you do not use.

OCCASIONAL

research (Matt)

Clean primary-source research flow with a committed Markdown artifact. Useful for ProjectAtlas-style competitive research.

ON DEMAND

grill-me (Matt)

The relentless-interview posture conflicts with your directive, unattended style. Keep it as an explicit tool for sharpening a spec before a big autonomous run, never as a default.

Do not adopt wholesale

COLLISIONS

Both full sets together

Both repositories define tdd and teach with materially different behavior. PStack also assumes Cursor paths, Cursor Task semantics, and Cursor model slugs throughout; unported installs will silently misfire.

REDUNDANT

swarm, setup-pstack, poteto-mode

Your pi workflows, Claude Workflow tool, and codex multi-agent config already cover orchestration. poteto-mode is another person's taste and too Cursor-bound; your own alex-mode (shortlist #5) replaces it.

NO DEMAND

Matt misc + in-progress

Shoehorn migration, exercise scaffolding, pre-commit setup, the writing suite, experimental handoff: no matching demand anywhere in the corpus.

TOO RIGID

no-comments, never-block-on-the-human

Fine as heuristics, wrong as hard policy. Your own CLAUDE.md explicitly wants clarifying questions when a task is unclear.

CEREMONY

to-tickets, triage, wayfinder (Matt)

Tracker-based flows that mutate external project state. No issue-tracker usage appears in any corpus; your planning artifacts are HTML pages on myhtmls.dev.
